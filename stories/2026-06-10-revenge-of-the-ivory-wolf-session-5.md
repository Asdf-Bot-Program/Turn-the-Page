Clan Agalor had settled into its particular rhythm of recovery, the kind of rhythm that exists when enough people have been in the same cramped space long enough to stop noticing how cramped it is and start noticing each other instead. Mia, Volg, and Lauren had their usual place around the fire alongside Murgakh and a few of the older clan members. Bayard, Ra'khadar, and Yashna continued their work on the cave-in with the steady efficiency of people who knew what needed to be done. Meanwhile, Yashna's stubborn gratitude grew in direct proportion to Bayard's exhaustion. Mevanos and Lorzub occupied their corner with the focused disorganization of people making good progress and being unable to fully prove it yet. Liliana and Gulraam had established a relationship with the kitchen that was functional for both parties, one producing, the other consuming, the arrangement requiring no further elaboration.
The evening was unremarkable until Mia noticed something. She had been going through the reports on shipments heading to Clan Agalor, the kind of administrative work that nobody glamorizes but that keeps a rebuilding clan from running out of materials. The pattern she found was not subtle once she saw it. Almost every shipment she had sent out was attacked immediately upon getting close to Clan Agalor. No deaths, which was the first thing worth noting, but a very small amount of what was sent was actually arriving. The same thing was happening to the supplies from Vos-Greel. She cross-referenced both sets of data and found two sources behind the disruptions: the white wolf, which was to be expected, and a mercenary group called the Grey Snow. Volg looked over her shoulder at the moment she arrived at this conclusion. His response was to stand up and put his hand on his sword, and he was already moving toward the exit when Morning came through it. The collision knocked Morning down while Volg stood confused about the interaction. Morning looked around at the assembled faces with the specific expression of someone carrying news that has been running her and needs to set it down immediately. She said that Clan Agalor had been challenged; someone called The Storm, the leader of the Grey Snow, had been in Orsinium for days, rallying support, making his case publicly. He was challenging Clan Agalor for their land, and because of how public the challenge had been made, refusing it was not a neutral act. It would be read as a concession and an invitation to every other clan in Wrothgar to treat the land as undefended.
They had to go, but she first asked for their presence; the more people they had, the better it would look for the clan. She went to collect the rest, Ra'khadar, Bayard, Gulraam, Mevanos, and Lorzub, stopping to check on Mevanos and Lorzub's research, which had made good progress. She asked Mevanos to return the Fighters Guild report to Olred when he had the opportunity, to maintain good relations with the Guild. She made the case to each of them that a Telvanni Magister, a Knight of Saint Pelin, and a Fighters Guild representative in the group's corner would say something to the people of Orsinium about who stood with Clan Agalor. Then she went to find Liliana, where she found her outside, standing in the cool mountain air with the particular stillness of someone who has put themselves there deliberately. Liliana said it plainly: the werewolf curse was getting harder to manage, and she was craving blood. Morning did not discourage this; she said that kind of state might be useful, that these things never turned out peaceful, and that she would be glad to bring Liliana along if she stayed close to Volg and let him help keep it in check until the moment required otherwise. Liliana accepted this as they all walked toward Orsinium.
The city received them in the way that cities receive people when everyone already knows why they are there. Strange looks ranging from mild curiosity to open hostility. Whispers from people in passing. They're finished. The Storm will sort this out. Did you see how few of them there are? Nobody tried to hide what they thought. Orsinium had already formed its opinion about how this was going to go. The group arrived at the staircase outside the Fighters Guild Hall and saw the large Orc man at the top of it, the crowd in front of him, and the performance already in progress. Morning and Evening stopped; they knew him. After more than fifteen years, across everything that had happened since the day they were separated from each other and from him, they knew him immediately, Yegoth, their father. Standing at the top of the stairs, working a crowd that had already decided to trust him, putting on the specific performance of a man who has earned the attention of a room and intends to use it. Despite the fear that arrived in their chests with the recognition, both of them kept moving. They pushed through the crowd with the group behind them and met him where he was performing. He was unimpressed. This was also a performance, but a different one, the arrogance of a man who has decided that what is standing in front of him does not deserve his full attention and is making sure the crowd knows it. He set the terms with the ease of someone who had already written them before arriving: three rounds, a one-on-one fight with The Storm himself, a race through a dungeon, and finally a hunt through the woods of Wrothgar. At the mention of the hunt, he looked at Evening specifically and said he doubted Evening could beat him at anything, given that he was the one who taught him everything he knew.
He delivered this to the crowd as much as to Evening, Morning stepped forward to shake on the terms. He took her arm and pulled her close rather than meeting her hand, and said it quietly enough to be private and loudly enough to be heard by the people immediately around them: "By the way, how's your little wolf problem?" He let her go and walked back into the Fighters Guild Hall with the crowd cheering behind him. Morning stood where she was for a moment with an expression of shock and awe. He knew. He had known about the white wolf before anyone had said it, which meant whatever was sending the Ivory Wolf toward Clan Agalor had a connection to the man who had just walked through that door. She turned back to the group and found Mevanos, and gave him the kind of look that is a full sentence. We need to figure out what that spirit is, and we need to do it now. Mevanos took the investigation group and headed out. Morning and Evening walked into the Fighters Guild Hall to discuss the specifics, afraid in ways they were not going to let show, to meet with their father properly for the first time since he was the reason they no longer had a family.
Mevanos, Lauren, and Bayard took the task; they returned the Fighters Guild report to Olred along with an update on what they had found, the kind of exchange that goes well when both parties are genuinely interested in the information, and Olred was. He thanked them and pointed them toward a Reach Witch he had been corresponding with, someone who had been closer to the oral tradition surrounding the spirit than anyone he had been able to reach through formal channels. They found the Hroldan Ring, a common area where various Reachman tribes intersected, the kind of place that exists between official territories and serves as its own kind of neutral ground. The Reach Witch Olred had described turned out to be someone they already knew, Granny Lexy Bortman. She was exactly as she had always been, senile but somehow wise. She had been waiting, in the specific way she always seemed to have been waiting, for exactly the people who showed up. She told them the story.
“Ya know of Lorkh, and how he went to the Spirit Queen Namiira, and how he asked for a place in the void for wayward spirits. The Hunt Father Hircine became the sovereign of Flesh, while the Queen of Death remained the ruler of the Spirit World. It is said by some that Lorkh still wanders the world in some form, acting for our people in great times of need. This tale features one of those times. The stories of our people have always been coveted by a greedy spirit. A great spirit of knowledge, the tides, and the woods. Herma Mora: the one who speaks in the wastes. He has long sought the knowledge of our Vateshrans and our secret rites. Herma Mora has taken many forms to trick us and test us. It is said by some “scholars” that we carry the blood of elven folk, that we, like the Bretons, are “tainted”. Ye will find nary a Vateshran that will admit this, but it is true. There is more to the story. In an age past, the Direnni, the elven clan, had come to our shores. Our people prayed to the Great Spirit Hircine for the strength to fight the mighty elven magicks. But it was not the Hunt Father who answered first. To trick our people, Herma Mora took the form of a wolf. A wolf with fur white as snow, but with haunting eyes. Pretending to be the Huntsman of the Princes, he said he would give us power in exchange for our stories. Some opposed, but some foolishly agreed. As they began gifting him with some of our secrets, there was a change. Our ears had become more pointed, our blood had changed with magicks." 
"Before we were fully transformed, a hare came forward. The hare revealed itself as Lorkh, and the wolf as Herma Mora. The wolf stopped wagging its tail and attacked the hare. The hare oozed blood black as night. The form had been killed. The wolf growled, angry at the hare for revealing its treachery, and it began feasting upon the corpse. But in came a fox with black fur and red eyes. The fox struck the wolf, killing it. The fox revealed itself as Hircine, chiding the people for falling for the wolf’s lies. It warned them that they would forever have the elven blood in their veins. Should they ever again reveal the secrets of our people to Herma Mora, they would not be welcome in the Spirit Queens' realm at death. Thus did our people refuse to write down our knowledge, and instead spoke our tales. You see, the blood of Lorkh is a mighty thing, connected to the void; it is a substance of great power. When the wolf consumed the hare and was struck down by the fox, something was born. Where the flesh of the wolf met the blood of the hare, something rose. A spirit. A spirit with a hunter's cunning. A spirit of vengeance and a spirit of balance. It is said this spirit lusts after revenge and will make sure deals are held, hunting down things it deems unjust. A combination of hunt, cunning, and flesh.” 
At the end, she spoke to each of them individually. What she said to Bayard and Mevanos is what it was. What she whispered to Lauren caused Lauren to leave immediately, quickly, in a direction that did not lead back to the group. Granny Lexy watched her go with the expression she uses when she already knows how something ends. Bayard and Mevanos made their way back to the rest of the Sugar Falls group in Wrothgar, carrying a story that had not been written down anywhere and could no longer be unheard.
